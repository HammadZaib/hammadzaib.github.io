Application Approved

Every form, every document, every long wait — complete.
The invitation has come, and it has your name on it.

✦

Of all the millions who long for it,
you were chosen.

Hajj is not something a person simply decides to do. It is an invitation — and it is only ever extended to those who are called. This year, out of all the hearts turned toward Makkah, yours was one of them.

I have watched you pray for this. I have watched you hope quietly, patiently, without ever demanding. And now the answer has arrived, exactly as it was always meant to.

I could not be prouder of you, or happier for you. Next year, insha’Allah, you will stand where the Prophets stood, walk where they walked, and answer the call in your own voice.

وَأَذِّن فِي النَّاسِ بِالْحَجِّ يَأْتُوكَ رِجَالًا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٍ يَأْتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٍ

“And proclaim to the people the Hajj — they will come to you” · Al-Hajj 22:27

The journey ahead

Insha’Allah, next year you will live every one of these.

1

Ihram إحرام

Two simple white cloths. Nothing to mark rank, wealth or origin — only a soul standing before its Lord.

2

Tawaf طواف

Seven circles around the Ka’bah, shoulder to shoulder with the whole world, everyone turning toward the same centre.

3

Sa’i سعي

Between Safa and Marwah — retracing the steps of Hajar, a mother who ran in trust and was answered with Zamzam.

4

Arafah عرفة

The heart of Hajj. A single afternoon on the plain, hands raised, where du’as are not turned away. Please remember me there.

5

Muzdalifah & Mina منى

A night under open sky, then the stoning at Jamarat — and after it, the lightness of a slate wiped clean.

“Here I am, O Allah, here I am.”
